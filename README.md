# BuildSync

### Construction Project Management & Contractor–Client Synchronization Platform

## Problem

Construction projects involve contractors, clients, labourers, and material suppliers. Project information is often scattered across WhatsApp messages, phone calls, notebooks, spreadsheets, and separate payment records.

This can lead to:
- Miscommunication between contractors and clients
- Difficulty tracking labour and material details
- Payment and expense confusion
- Poor visibility of project progress
- Loss of important project information

## Solution

BuildSync is a centralized construction project management platform that connects contractors and clients in one application.

Contractors can:
- Create and manage their business profile
- Search and connect with clients using usernames
- Upload project designs and site photos
- Manage labour and payment details
- Record materials, quantities, and costs
- Communicate with clients through chat

All relevant information entered by the contractor is synchronized with the client's account, allowing the client to easily view project updates, labour, materials, photos, designs, and communication.

### Core Workflow

Contractor → BuildSync → Client

Contractor adds project information → Information is synchronized → Client views updated information
